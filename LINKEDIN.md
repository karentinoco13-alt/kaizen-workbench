## Post

Waste always gets paid for. Sometimes in money. Sometimes in customers who wait. And often in people who stop paying attention to detail because their work no longer seems to matter.

That includes the waste we rarely call waste: over-planning, over-staffing, and projects with no clear goal, no deadline and no real problem to solve.

So I built Kaizen Workbench, a free process-improvement app for managers. It brings together the methods that have worked best for decades:

🔹 Amazon's Working Backwards: start from the customer need
🔹 A funding gate: every project matched to a need, funded on data, with a pilot plan
🔹 Toyota value stream mapping and the 8 wastes, with a "So what?" ladder that puts a dollar cost on each one
🔹 The 5 Whys to reach the real root cause
🔹 Pilot, then scale or stop, and lock in the gains with standard work
🔹 A built-in SOP that teaches teams to use it step by step
🔹 Fully bilingual: English and Spanish

The principle behind it: everything in an organization must add value. People, resources and systems should work together to produce it.

Try it here (no sign-up, and your data stays in your browser): https://karentinoco13-alt.github.io/kaizen-workbench/
